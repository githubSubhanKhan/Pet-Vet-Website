Caring for Long Island's wildlife, too

Massapequa Pet Vet sponsors Long Island Wildlife & Animal Rescue (LIWAR), a not-for-profit 501(c)(3). Our doctors work alongside more than ten licensed rehabilitators to treat injured native animals, and together we save hundreds of them every year.

Ask how you can help

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Call us at (516) 797-8387.

What should I do if my pet needs help after hours?

Call 631.587.0800. During office hours, call us first at (516) 797-8387 and we'll do our best to see your pet the same day.

Do you see exotic pets?

Yes. We care for birds, rabbits, ferrets and other small mammals, reptiles, and fish and aquatic animals, alongside dogs and cats.

Can I refill my pet's prescription online?

Yes. Use the refill request form, or order medications and food for delivery from our online pharmacy.

How do I send or request my pet's records?

Email reception@mpetvet.com or fax us at 516.798.3061. You can also see vaccine history in the pet portal.
